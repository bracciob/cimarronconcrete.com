Removal & Replacement

When a driveway, patio, sidewalk or slab is cracked, heaved or sinking, patching only buys time. We tear it out, fix what caused the problem underneath, and replace it with new concrete built to last.

One crew handles the whole job, from breaking out the old concrete to the final finish, so there is no hand-off between contractors.

How it works

Removal & replacement, step by step.

  1. 01

    Break out the old concrete

    Damaged concrete is saw cut at clean lines, broken up and hauled away, so the rest of your property stays untouched.

  2. 02

    Fix the base

    Most failures start underneath. We regrade, add and compact new base material and set forms to the right grade for drainage.

  3. 03

    Pour & finish new

    Fresh reinforced concrete is placed and finished to match or upgrade what was there, with control joints where they belong.

  4. 04

    Driveways, patios, walks & slabs

    Residential and commercial: driveways, patios, sidewalks, steps, garage and shop slabs.

Questions

Good to know.

Should I repair or replace my concrete?

Small hairline cracks can often be left alone. If slabs have heaved, sunk, broken into pieces or hold water, replacement is usually the lasting fix. We will tell you honestly which one you need.

Do you haul away the old concrete?

Yes. Removal, hauling and disposal are part of the job.

Can you replace just one section?

Yes. We can saw cut and replace a single panel or section of a driveway, sidewalk or patio instead of the whole thing.

How soon can I use it again?

Foot traffic is usually fine after a day or two. Wait about a week before driving on a new driveway.
