Questions

Good to know.

Can you remove and replace my old driveway or patio?

Yes. Demolition and removal of existing concrete is part of many of our residential jobs.

When can I drive or walk on new concrete?

Foot traffic is usually fine within a day or two, and vehicles typically wait about a week. We will confirm the timing for your project.

Why does my concrete need control joints?

Concrete shrinks as it cures. Joints cut in the right places guide that movement so the slab cracks where you want it to, not across the middle.
