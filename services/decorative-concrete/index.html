Questions

Good to know.

Can I see pattern and color options?

Yes. We will walk through patterns, colors and borders with you before anything is poured.

Is stamped concrete slippery?

Texture and finish choices make a big difference. We will recommend a finish that suits the area, especially around pools and steps.

Can you add steps and curves to a stamped patio?

Yes. Custom steps, rounded edges and borders are some of our favorite details.
