What we build

Foundations, in detail.

  1. 01

    Foundation walls

    Formed and braced walls with rebar placed to plan, poured in one continuous placement for homes, additions and commercial structures.

  2. 02

    Monolithic slabs

    Slab and footing placed together in a single pour, an efficient foundation that is strong, level and ready for framing.

  3. 03

    Slabs & pads

    Structural pads for garages, shops, equipment and building additions, with a compacted base and reinforcement sized for the load.

  4. 04

    Residential & commercial

    The same discipline on a single home pad or a large commercial footprint, from footings to the final finish.

How it runs

From first visit to final finish.

  1. 01

    Excavation, footings and layout checked against the plans.

  2. 02

    Concrete placed and consolidated, walls and slabs finished.

    Forms stripped, concrete cured and the site left clean.

Questions

Good to know.

Do you handle both residential and commercial foundations?

Yes. Cimarron Concrete builds foundation walls, monolithic slabs and pads for homes, additions, shops and commercial buildings.

Can you work from my engineer's or builder's plans?

Yes. Send the plans with your quote request and we will build the layout, forms and reinforcement to them.

How soon can framing start after the pour?

It depends on the mix, the weather and the engineer's requirements. We will give you a clear timeline for your job when we schedule the pour.
