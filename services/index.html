How a project runs

From estimate to final finish.

  1. 01

    Estimate

    We walk the property, look at slope, drainage and access, and give you a clear, detailed estimate.

  2. 02

    Site prep

    Demolition and removal where needed, excavation, grading and a compacted base.

  3. 03

    Forms & steel

    Forms set square and to grade, with reinforcement placed for the load the concrete will carry.

  4. 04

    Pour & finish

    Placed, screeded and finished to the texture you chose, with updates on weather and pour dates.

  5. 05

    Cure & clean up

    Joints cut, forms stripped and the site left clean, with results you can count on.

Questions

Good to know.

Don't see your question? Call or text (970) 388-2222.

What areas do you serve?

Northern Colorado and the Denver Metro, including Fort Collins, Windsor, Severance, Greeley, Boulder, Broomfield, Brighton and Cheyenne, Wyoming.

How do I get an estimate?

Request a quote online or call or text us. We will walk the property, look at slope, drainage and access, and give you a clear, detailed estimate.

Do you remove old concrete?

Yes. Demolition and removal of existing concrete is part of many of our jobs.

Do you take commercial work?

Yes. We build commercial slabs, site work, light pole bases, right-of-way installations, valley pans and stairs.

What happens if weather affects my pour date?

Concrete is weather sensitive. We keep you updated on any weather that could affect progress or pour dates, and reschedule when needed so the finished work is right.
