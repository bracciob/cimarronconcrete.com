Owner & Founder

Meet Marco

When you hire Cimarron Concrete, you are working with someone who truly knows the business from the ground up.

Marco brings 20 years of real, hands-on experience in the concrete trade to every project. His journey is built on a solid foundation, starting as a laborer and mastering every stage of the craft as a skilled finisher, crew foreman, and site superintendent before launching his own company. Having worked through every single role in the field, Marco offers a true "boots on the ground" perspective that ensures every job is executed safely, efficiently, and to the highest standard.

Marco's mission is simple: deliver exceptional quality and craftsmanship to every client. He understands the craft inside and out, and his sharp attention to detail and genuine passion for the work set Cimarron Concrete apart in the industry. He takes immense pride in doing things right the first time, giving clients results they can count on.
